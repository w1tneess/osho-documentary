The 1984 Rajneeshee bioterror attack was the intentional contamination of salad bars at ten restaurants in The Dalles, Oregon, with _Salmonella typhimurium_. The attack was orchestrated by Ma Anand Sheela and other leaders of the <GlossaryTerm id="rajneeshpuram" text="Rajneeshpuram" /> commune in a bid to influence a local election.

. At the time, it was the largest biological terrorist attack in United States history

## The Plot

Tensions between the residents of Rajneeshpuram and the local population of Wasco County had reached a breaking point by 1984. Seeking to gain political control of the county by suppressing voter turnout on election day, Sheela's inner circle devised a plan to sicken voters <Cite id="carter-2011" />.

The group first acquired _Salmonella_ bacteria from a medical supplier and cultured it in a laboratory within the commune. As a "trial run," they began contaminating local restaurant salad bars and water supplies in The Dalles starting in September 1984 <Cite id="oregon-ag-investigation" />.

## Discovery and Investigation

The outbreak was initially investigated by the CDC, which did not immediately suspect foul play. However, after Sheela fled the commune in September 1985, Osho broke his silence and publicly accused her of orchestrating the attacks <Cite id="usdoj-cr85-0150" />.

Subsequent raids by the FBI and Oregon State Police discovered a clandestine biological warfare laboratory and a glass vial containing a _Salmonella_ culture that genetically matched the strain from the outbreak <Cite id="oregonian-series" />.

## Convictions

In 1986, Ma Anand Sheela and Dr. Diane Yvonne Onang (Ma Anand Puja) pleaded guilty to charges related to the attack, among other crimes. <Claim level="established" cite={["usdoj-cr85-0150"]}>They were sentenced to concurrent prison terms, though both served only a fraction of their sentences before being released and deported</Claim>.
