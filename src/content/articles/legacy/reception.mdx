Osho Rajneesh presents a profound historical and psychological paradox: a brilliant philosopher of consciousness who was simultaneously morally careless and organizationally abusive.

More than three decades after his death, his legacy remains intensely polarized.

## The Survival of the Movement

Unlike many new religious movements that collapse after the death of their charismatic founder, the Osho movement survived and adapted.

The ashram in Pune was transformed into the OSHO International Meditation Resort. Today, it operates more like a spiritual spa or corporate retreat center than a traditional ashram. <Claim level="established" cite={["urban-2015"]}>Thousands of visitors from around the world continue to travel to Pune annually to participate in meditation and therapy programs</Claim>.

Osho's intellectual output was staggering. His transcribed discourses have been translated into over 60 languages and compiled into hundreds of books, which continue to sell millions of copies worldwide <Cite id="osho-com-archives" />. His techniques, particularly <GlossaryTerm id="dynamic-meditation" text="Dynamic Meditation" /> and Kundalini Meditation, are practiced in independent meditation centers across the globe.

## The Paradox of the Master

To objectively assess Osho is to hold two contradictory truths simultaneously.

<Claim level="interpretation" cite={['urban-2015', 'goleman-davidson-2017']}>
  On one hand, his philosophical deconstruction of religious dogma was sharp and necessary
</Claim>
. He recognized early that sexual repression causes psychological damage. His emphasis on direct
experience over inherited belief, and his creation of active meditations to bypass modern neurosis,
have been empirically validated as beneficial.

<Claim level="interpretation" cite={['hassan-2015', 'carter-2011']}>
  On the other hand, he propagated the dangerous myth that enlightenment confers moral infallibility
</Claim>
. By demanding absolute surrender and establishing a culture without democratic accountability or
ethical boundaries, he created the exact conditions necessary for abuse and corruption.

For modern seekers and historians, Osho serves as both a masterclass in spiritual philosophy and a profound warning about the dangers of unchecked charismatic authority.
