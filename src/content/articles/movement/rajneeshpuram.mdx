On June 1, 1981, Osho left India for the United States. The move was orchestrated by his personal secretary, Ma Anand Sheela, an Indian-American woman with a talent for organization and strong ambition <Cite id="carter-2011" />.

In August 1981, Sheela negotiated the purchase of a 64,229-acre ranch in Wasco County, Oregon, for $5.75 million. The property, formerly known as the Big Muddy Ranch, was intended to be a new utopian civilization <Cite id="fitzgerald-1986" />.

## A New Civilization

The community was renamed <GlossaryTerm id="rajneeshpuram" text="Rajneeshpuram" /> and successfully incorporated as a city in May 1982. The development was rapid and massive.

At its peak between 1983 and 1984, <Claim level="established" cite={["fitzgerald-1986", "oregonian-series"]}>Rajneeshpuram had approximately 7,000 residents</Claim>. The community built schools, farms, factories, a medical center, its own police force, fire department, and an airport <Cite id="fitzgerald-1986" />.

> This commune is not an ordinary society; it is an experiment in consciousness, a laboratory of human transformation. We are trying to create an oasis in the desert where human beings can live without fear, without the past, and without greed.

During this period, Osho lived in strict seclusion. <Claim level="established" cite={["carter-2011"]}>Sheela became the de facto leader, running day-to-day operations with increasing authoritarian control</Claim>. Osho communicated primarily through Sheela, demanding complete surrender and obedience from his followers.

## Tensions with Local Residents

The rapid influx of <GlossaryTerm id="sannyasin" text="sannyasins" /> caused immediate tension with the local residents of Wasco County. Concerns over land use, zoning laws, and the commune's aggressive legal tactics led to fierce opposition from groups like "1000 Friends of Oregon" <Cite id="oregonian-series" />.

The conflict escalated into attempts to influence local elections, culminating in the 1984 Salmonella bioterror attack in The Dalles (see <a href="/articles/controversies/bioterror-attack">Bioterror Attack</a>).

## Collapse and Federal Intervention

In September 1985, Sheela and several members of her inner circle abruptly fled the commune for Europe. Osho broke his silence, publicly expelled her, and encouraged followers to report her crimes to the authorities <Cite id="usdoj-cr85-0150" />.

Subsequent criminal investigations by state and federal authorities revealed immigration fraud, wiretapping, and the bioterror attack. By November 1985, Osho was arrested on immigration fraud charges <Cite id="oregon-ag-investigation" />.
