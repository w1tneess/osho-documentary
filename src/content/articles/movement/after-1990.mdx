Following Osho’s death on January 19, 1990, the leadership of the movement transitioned to an inner council of long-term disciples established to manage the ashram in Pune and the intellectual property of his discourses <Cite id="carter-2011" />.

The Pune ashram underwent extensive architectural remodeling, rebranding itself as the **Osho International Meditation Resort**, adopting contemporary wellness, spa, and hospitality models rather than traditional monastic or commune paradigms <Cite id="urban-2015" />.

  The Zurich-based Osho International Foundation (OIF) asserted centralized copyright and trademark ownership over Osho’s written works, audio recordings, and name.
</Claim>

## The Digital Discourse Archives

A primary focus of the post-1990 movement became the comprehensive preservation and digital dissemination of Osho’s spoken-word record. Today, two major digital archives preserve this corpus:

- **<a href="https://www.osho.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">OSHO.com (Osho International)</a>:** The official multimedia platform featuring digital book catalogs, OSHO Online Library, iOS/Android apps, and video streaming of historical discourses.
- **<a href="https://oshoworld.com/" target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer">Osho World (oshoworld.com)</a>:** An extensive open-access archive based in New Delhi, hosting thousands of hours of unedited MP3 discourse audio in Hindi and English, original magazine archives, and photographic records.

## Intellectual Property and Schisms

During the 2000s and 2010s, significant legal controversies erupted between the OIF board in Switzerland and independent sannyasins worldwide who contested the commercialization and trademarking of the word "Osho" <Cite id="urban-2015" />.

In the United States, the Trademark Trial and Appeal Board (TTAB) in 2009 ruled against OIF's exclusive trademark claim to the term "Osho," finding that the name had become generic for the spiritual movement and teachings rather than a proprietary brand identifier.

Today, the movement operates as a decentralized network of independent meditation centers alongside the central Pune resort, with discourse publishing translated into dozens of languages across global publishing houses.
